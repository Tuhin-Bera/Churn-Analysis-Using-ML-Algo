Step 2: EDA & Feature Engineering

Exploratory Insights & Domain Features

EDA revealed strong contract and tenure bifurcations. We engineered 13 domain-specific signals to boost predictive accuracy.

🔍 Critical EDA Discoveries

• Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7% vs 2.8% for Two-Year contracts.
• Tenure < 12 months represents 47% churn risk.
• Fiber Optic users churn twice as often as DSL (likely service quality/expectations gap).

Step 4: Survival Analysis

Lifelines: Kaplan-Meier & Cox Hazard Model

Accounting for right-censoring to predict continuous survival curves and hazard ratios over 72 months of customer lifecycle.

📈 Kaplan-Meier Findings

• Overall Survival: 6m = 91%, 12m = 80%, 24m = 71%, 48m = 61%
• Contract Stratification: Log-rank test p < 0.0001 confirms statistical significance between Month-to-Month and multi-year retention curves.
• Two-year contract customers exhibit over 95% 24-month survival probability.

Step 6: CLV & Clustering

Discounted CLV & K-Means Segmentation

Segmenting 7,043 customers by lifetime value, churn risk, tenure, and engagement into 4 actionable business segments.

💎 Discounted Cash Flow CLV

CLV = MonthlyCharges * 30% Margin * [(1 - (1+r)^-N) / r]
Where r = 10%/12 and N is predicted by the Cox model.

• Mean CLV: ₹835 | Max CLV: ₹1,923
• Top 10% Customers: Generate 28% of total portfolio value.

💡 Key Learnings & Ownership

• Causal Inference > Predictive Accuracy: Predicting who will leave is trivial compared to identifying who will change behavior upon intervention.
• Survival Analysis is Vital: Binary classification ignores customer longevity; survival curves unlock time-based CLV calculations.
• Production Readiness: Dual database architecture (Postgres + SQLite) ensures zero-friction local development and deployment.
